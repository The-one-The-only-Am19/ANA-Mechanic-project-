My rating: 8 / 10

I rate ANA Mechanic Shop an 8 out of 10 for workplace health and safety. The shop keeps safety equipment where people need it, with fire extinguishers near the bay exits and an eyewash station beside the hazardous waste area. The linear workflow sends each car in through the bay door and out the same way, so customers stay away from the work area. Hazardous waste and batteries are stored away from the break room and waiting area, which lowers the chance of chemical exposure. A PPE station at the tool room entrance makes it easy to grab glasses, gloves, and ear protection before starting work. The biggest risks are the vehicle lifts and power tools, so teens should only use them with training and supervision. Noise from the compressor and exhaust fumes are easy to forget because they build up slowly. The shop could improve with anti-slip floor mats, marked walkways, and regular emergency drills. Teen workers should also get a written list of tasks they are not allowed to do, such as running certain lifts or driving customer cars. With those changes and strict use of PPE, this shop could earn a 10 out of 10 for young workers.
